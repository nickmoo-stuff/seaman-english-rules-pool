YOUR DATA

Privacy and data

Seamen English Rules Pool does not currently require an account and does not contain advertising or analytics.

Stored on this device

Your career progress, statistics, achievements, pirate progression, cosmetic choices, tutorial state and preferences are stored locally by your browser or installed PWA. On Android, Supporter Pack ownership is checked through Google Play, with a temporary confirmation kept in memory. Local saves and settings do not establish paid ownership.

Save files

Export save creates a JSON file containing portable progress and settings so you can keep a backup or move them to another browser or device. Supporter entitlements are deliberately not included in exported saves.

Offline storage and network use

The PWA may cache the game code, icons and some artwork on this device so core gameplay can work offline. Soundtrack files are not deliberately cached and may be requested from the game's host while you are online. The game also checks its own host for updated game files. External rules, music and credit links are only opened when you choose them.

Reset player data

Reset player data removes career statistics, trophies, pirate progress, equipped/unlocked career cosmetics and first-run/tutorial progress from this browser. Audio settings and Aim guide preferences are intentionally kept. Supporter entitlements are separate and are not removed by a career reset.

Removing the installed PWA or clearing this site's browser/app data is controlled by your browser or operating system and is separate from the in-game career reset.

How break points work

3 cumulative points are required for a legal break.

• 1 point for each object ball potted, including the black.
• 1 point for each unpotted object ball whose whole ball crosses the imaginary line between the two centre pockets.
• A ball can only score once, even if it crosses the line and is then potted.

If fewer than 3 points are scored, the balls are re-racked and the opponent chooses who breaks next.
