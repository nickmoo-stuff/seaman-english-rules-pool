How break points work

3 cumulative points are required for a legal break.

• 1 point for each object ball potted, including the black.
• 1 point for each unpotted object ball whose whole ball crosses the imaginary line between the two centre pockets.
• A ball can only score once, even if it crosses the line and is then potted.

If fewer than 3 points are scored, the balls are re-racked and the opponent chooses who breaks next.
