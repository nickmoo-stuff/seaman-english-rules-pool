## Native review decisions

Fresh Play inventory is the chosen device authority. Acknowledgement is required before granting a volatile owner session; errors and cache lag do not grant. A successful notification alone is insufficient. Concurrent ownership checks share one state machine. SDK calls are bounded, old connection generations are fenced, and disconnect events invalidate without an immediate reconnect-query loop. A persisted, network-required OS retry job stores no token or owner flag. Purchase launch remains behind a native compile-time false gate; the implemented launch path is tested with fake transport only.

See [security limitations and future backend path](native-patches/K3H.1/SECURITY.md). This model does not independently verify signatures or Developer API tokens and cannot resist modified clients. OS acknowledgement retry is best effort and can miss Google's acknowledgement deadline when force-stopped/offline; no server reliability guarantee is claimed.
